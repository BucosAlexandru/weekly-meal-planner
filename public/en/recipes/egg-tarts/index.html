Egg Tarts

The egg tart is a hybrid creation from the former Portuguese colony of Macau, where local cooks adapted the Portuguese pastéis de nata — with its puff pastry and caramelized custard top — to Cantonese tastes, dropping the scorched surface and lightening the custard. From there the recipe passed into Hong Kong, where it became a pillar of cha chaan teng culture (Hong Kong-style diners) and a symbol of a local cuisine in its own right, distinct from both the Portuguese original and Western custard tarts. What separates a successful tart from a mediocre one comes down to a few technical details: the custard has to be strained several times to remove air bubbles, or the surface fills with dimples during baking; the initial high heat quickly sets the pastry shell, and the reduced heat afterward lets the custard coagulate gently without cracking; and the tart is pulled from the oven while the center is still slightly wobbly, since it keeps cooking from its own residual heat after coming out.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 1 pack puff pastry (450g), fridge-cold
  • 150g sugar
  • 180ml boiling water
  • 180ml evaporated milk
  • 4 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • A pinch of salt

How to make it

  1. 1PREPARE THE SYRUP: dissolve the sugar in the boiling water, stirring until fully melted, then let cool to room temperature.
  2. 2SHAPE THE SHELLS: cut the puff pastry into squares, press each piece into a small tart mold, lifting the edges above the rim.
  3. 3MIX THE CUSTARD: whisk the eggs lightly with the evaporated milk, the cooled syrup, vanilla and salt, without incorporating air bubbles.
  4. 4STRAIN: pass the custard through a fine sieve 2-3 times, for a completely smooth texture with no lumps.
  5. 5FILL THE SHELLS: pour the custard into the pastry shells, up to about three-quarters of the height, so it doesn't overflow while baking.
  6. 6BAKE: bake at 220°C for 10 minutes, then reduce to 180°C and bake for another 10-12 minutes, until the custard sets but the center is still slightly wobbly.
  7. 7COOL AND SERVE: let the tarts rest 5 minutes in the mold before removing, then serve them warm.
