Apfelstrudel

Though considered one of the symbols of Austrian cuisine, strudel has roots that reach much further east: the technique of stretching dough to paper-thinness reached Vienna via the Ottoman Empire, from baklava and similar layered-dough techniques of Turkish-Arab cuisines, carried into Central Europe through trade and migration across the 16th-17th centuries. Strudel dough is distinguished from French puff pastry or pie dough by the absence of laminated butter layers — the thinness comes purely from hand-stretching, not folding. Stretching the dough is considered the ultimate test of a Viennese pastry cook: it's traditionally said the dough must be thin enough to read a newspaper through it, without tearing. The gluten in the flour needs to be sufficiently developed through long kneading for the dough to withstand stretching, but the minimum 30-minute rest is just as essential — it relaxes the gluten and stops it from elastically snapping back during stretching.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp vinegar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1.2kg tart apples, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/3 cup raisins
  • 1/3 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 150g melted butter
  • Powdered sugar, for serving

How to make it

  1. 1KNEAD THE DOUGH: Mix the flour with the warm water, oil, vinegar and salt, kneading an elastic dough for 10 minutes, until smooth and soft.
  2. 2LET IT REST: Brush the dough with a little oil, cover it and let it rest at least 30 minutes at room temperature.
  3. 3PREPARE THE BREADCRUMBS: Toast the breadcrumbs in 3 tablespoons of melted butter, over low heat, stirring often, until golden.
  4. 4STRETCH THE DOUGH: On a large tablecloth dusted with flour, stretch the dough by hand, gently lifting it from underneath the edges, until it's so thin you can read through it.
  5. 5BRUSH WITH BUTTER: Brush the whole surface of the dough with melted butter.
  6. 6SPRINKLE THE BREADCRUMBS: Sprinkle the toasted breadcrumbs evenly over two-thirds of the dough, closer to one edge.
  7. 7ADD THE FILLING: Mix the sliced apples with the sugar, cinnamon, raisins and walnuts, then spread the mixture over the breadcrumb layer.
  8. 8ROLL: Using the tablecloth as a guide, lift the breadcrumb edge and roll the strudel tightly, letting the cloth do the work, without grabbing the dough directly.
  9. 9PLACE ON THE TRAY: Carefully move the roll onto a tray lined with baking paper, seam side down, and tuck the ends underneath.
  10. 10BRUSH AND BAKE: Brush the surface with the remaining melted butter and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 35-40 minutes, until golden and crisp.
  11. 11SERVE: Let it cool slightly, dust with powdered sugar, and cut into thick slices.
