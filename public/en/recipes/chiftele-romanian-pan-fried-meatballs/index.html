Chiftele (Romanian Pan-Fried Meatballs)

Chiftele are one of the most common home dishes in Romanian cooking, appearing at almost every Sunday table alongside mashed potatoes or pickles. The name comes from Turkish, via the Balkan route, but the recipe has long since adapted to local ingredients — pork and beef, bread instead of rice, simple seasonings. What separates home-style chiftele from a mediocre version is the shape and the interior texture: flattened rather than round, so they cook evenly through the middle without burning on the outside; and bread soaked in milk rather than dry breadcrumbs, which keeps the meat juicy without weighing it down. The amount of oil matters just as much — too little and the meatballs dry out on the bottom before browning, too much and they come out greasy.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 500g mixed pork and beef mince
  • 2 slices white bread
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Oil, for frying

How to make it

  1. 1SOAK THE BREAD: tear the bread slices into pieces and let them sit in the milk for 5 minutes, until fully softened.
  2. 2Squeeze out the excess milk by hand.
  3. 3COMBINE THE MIXTURE: put the minced meat in a large bowl, add the squeezed bread, egg, onion, garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
  4. 4KNEAD BY HAND: mix all the ingredients well by hand for 3-4 minutes, until the mixture becomes even and slightly sticky.
  5. 5LET IT REST: cover the bowl and refrigerate the mixture for 20-30 minutes, so the flavours combine and the meat binds better.
  6. 6SHAPE THE MEATBALLS: wet your hands with water and shape golf-ball-sized balls, then flatten them gently between your palms.
  7. 7HEAT THE OIL: pour oil into a large pan to a depth of 1-1.5cm and heat over medium heat.
  8. 8FRY: add the meatballs to the pan without crowding and fry for 4-5 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through.
  9. 9DRAIN AND SERVE: transfer the meatballs to paper towel to remove excess oil, then serve hot.
