Lahmacun

Lahmacun, whose name comes from Arabic and literally means "meat with dough", is considered by many the direct precursor of pizza — merchants and migrants along the trade routes of Anatolia and the Levant carried and adapted the dish over centuries, long before the tomato-topped dough baked in an oven became an Italian symbol. Today it remains one of the most widespread street snacks in Turkey, sold over the counter, rolled up and eaten on the go, not served sitting at a table. The detail that separates an authentic lahmacun from a simple thin pizza is the extreme thinness of the dough — much thinner than any Italian pizza, almost translucent before baking. This minimal thickness, combined with the very high baking temperature, lets the meat topping, spread just as thin, dry out and brown in just a few minutes, without the center of the dough staying underbaked. Equally important is the timing of the herbs: they're added only when rolling, right before eating, not before baking, so they stay raw and fresh, in contrast with the warm, spiced topping.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 300g flour
  • 1 tsp dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 180ml lukewarm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g beef or lamb, very finely minced
  • 1 small onion, very finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, very finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, very finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • Fresh parsley and lemon, for serving

How to make it

  1. 1PREPARE THE DOUGH: dissolve the yeast and sugar in the lukewarm water, let sit 5 minutes, then mix with the flour, salt and oil, knead an elastic dough, let rise covered 30-40 minutes.
  2. 2PREPARE THE TOPPING: mix the finely minced meat with the onion, tomatoes, bell pepper, tomato paste and paprika, into a uniform paste.
  3. 3DIVIDE AND ROLL: divide the dough into 8 pieces, roll each very thin, almost translucent.
  4. 4SPREAD: spread a thin, even layer of the topping over the whole dough, right to the edges.
  5. 5BAKE: bake at 250°C, on a hot tray or stone, 5-7 minutes, until the edges brown and the topping dries out.
  6. 6SERVE: squeeze a little lemon on top, add fresh parsley, roll up and serve immediately, while hot.
