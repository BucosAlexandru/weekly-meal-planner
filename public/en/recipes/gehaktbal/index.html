Gehaktbal

Gehaktbal represents the Dutch's simple, hearty version of the meatball, distinguished by its generous size — a single large ball of meat per portion, unlike small meatballs served three or four at a time. The recipe reflects the Dutch culinary tradition of abundant, unpretentious dishes designed to satisfy workers after a long day. Unlike many neighboring cuisines that marinate or heavily spice the meat, gehaktbal remains deliberately simple, seasoned only with salt, pepper and a little onion, letting the taste of quality meat dominate. It is considered one of the most representative dishes of Dutch home cooking, almost always served with potatoes and boiled vegetables, a combination familiarly called «vlees, aardappelen, groente».

One large meatball per portion

Unlike small meatballs, gehaktbal is a single large ball of meat, about the size of a fist, cooked as one portion.

🥄

A mix of beef and pork

The ground meat combines beef and pork in equal proportions, giving a balance between flavor and juiciness.

🍞

Breadcrumbs and milk for tenderness

Breadcrumbs soaked in milk are mixed into the meat, keeping the meatballs tender inside after frying.

🍯

A thick gravy from the frying juices

The juices left in the pan after frying become a thick, savory gravy, served directly over the meatball.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 300g ground beef
  • 300g ground pork
  • 1 slice bread, crust removed
  • 100ml milk
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 200ml beef stock
  • Salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. 1Soak the slice of bread in milk for five minutes, then squeeze out the excess liquid, keeping the bread moist.
  2. 2Mix the two types of ground meat with the soaked bread, chopped onion, egg, salt and pepper in a large bowl.
  3. 3Knead the mixture by hand for a few minutes, until uniform, then shape into four large, equal balls.
  4. 4Melt the butter in a heavy skillet over medium heat and brown the meatballs on all sides for eight minutes.
  5. 5Reduce the heat to low, cover the pan and cook for twenty minutes, turning occasionally.
  6. 6Remove the meatballs and set them aside, then pour the beef stock into the pan, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom.
  7. 7Let the sauce reduce for a few minutes until it thickens, then pour it over the meatballs before serving.
