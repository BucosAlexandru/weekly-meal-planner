Flavours of China

The cuisine of China is built on rice, soy, ginger, sesame and umami-rich broths. The 15 recipes gathered on this page lean on those everyday building blocks, so you can bring an authentic taste of China to your own kitchen without hunting down hard-to-find ingredients or specialist equipment. Each one keeps the techniques approachable while staying true to the way the dish is traditionally made.
