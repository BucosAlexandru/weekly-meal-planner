Patatas Bravas

Patatas bravas were born in the taverns of Madrid in the mid-20th century, as an after-hours snack for workers stopping for a beer after their shift — the name "bravas" means "wild" or "fierce", a direct reference to the spicy sauce that accompanies them. They're now one of the most ordered tapas in Spain, though the exact version of the sauce remains a fiercely disputed topic between regions: Madrid prefers a red, paprika-based version, while Catalonia often adds a separate white alioli alongside. The secret to good patatas bravas lies in the two fries at different temperatures — a technique borrowed from classic French fries — which leave the inside fluffy and the outside extremely crispy. Equally important is the moment the sauce is poured: a patatas bravas left sitting too long in sauce loses exactly what makes it special, the crispy texture, turning into a soft dish closer to a stew, instead of the rustic snack, with a strong texture contrast, it's known for.

The potatoes are cut into irregular chunks, not even slices

The sharp edges of hand-broken or roughly cut chunks catch more crispy crust when fried

💧

The chunks soak in cold water before frying, then dry well

Removes excess surface starch, so frying gives crispness, not stickiness

🌶️

The brava sauce cooks separately, with smoked paprika, not with ketchup or store-bought hot sauce

Smoked paprika gives a smoky depth no ready-made sauce can imitate

🥣

The sauce is poured over the potatoes right before serving, not during frying

The potatoes stay crisp on the outside, instead of softening while sitting in sauce

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and cut into irregular chunks
  • Oil, for frying
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, very finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp smoked sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tsp hot paprika
  • 400g canned diced tomatoes
  • 1 tsp sherry vinegar or white wine vinegar
  • Salt, to taste

How to make it

  1. 1SOAK THE POTATOES: put the potato chunks in cold water for 15-20 minutes, then drain and dry them well with a towel.
  2. 2PREPARE THE SAUCE: heat the olive oil, sauté the onion until soft, add the garlic, then the sweet and hot paprika, stir quickly.
  3. 3ADD THE TOMATOES: add the diced tomatoes, simmer 15-20 minutes, until the sauce thickens, then blend it until smooth, add the vinegar and salt.
  4. 4FRY THE POTATOES: heat the frying oil to 170°C, fry the potatoes in batches 8-10 minutes, until lightly browned.
  5. 5SECOND FRY: raise the oil temperature to 190°C, fry the potatoes again 2-3 minutes, until golden and crisp.
  6. 6SERVE: drain the potatoes on paper towels, salt them, place on a platter and pour the brava sauce over right before serving.
