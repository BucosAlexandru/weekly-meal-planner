Mercimek Çorbası

Mercimek çorbası is present at almost every Turkish table, from the simplest everyday kitchen to the fixed menu traditionally used to break the fast during Ramadan. Its simplicity — lentils, a few common vegetables, water — makes it an accessible dish for any budget, which is why it's rarely missing from a Turkish restaurant's menu, regardless of its level. Choosing split red lentils, over any other variety, is essential to the final result: already split, they break down completely in 20-25 minutes of boiling, without needing prior soaking like green or brown lentils. The paprika-infused butter, melted separately and poured on top only at serving, doesn't just color the soup — the step of cooking it briefly, over low heat, develops the paprika's flavor without burning it, a detail that would be lost if the paprika were added directly into the soup pot from the start.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 250g split red lentils
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 small potato, chopped
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 liter vegetable broth or water
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 tsp sweet paprika (for the butter)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Lemon juice, for serving
  • Dried mint, for serving

How to make it

  1. 1PREPARE THE SOUP: put the lentils, onion, carrot, garlic, potato and sweet paprika in a large pot, add the water or broth.
  2. 2BOIL: bring to a boil, then simmer 20-25 minutes, until the lentils and vegetables are fully tender and falling apart.
  3. 3BLEND: blend everything (with an immersion or upright blender), until a smooth cream.
  4. 4SEASON: taste and add salt and pepper, add more water if the soup is too thick.
  5. 5PREPARE THE FLAVORED BUTTER: melt the butter in a small saucepan, add the sweet paprika, stir a few seconds over low heat, until colored, without burning.
  6. 6SERVE: pour the soup into bowls, drizzle the paprika butter over each bowl, serve with lemon juice and dried mint on the side.
