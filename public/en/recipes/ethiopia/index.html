Flavours of Ethiopia

The cuisine of Ethiopia is built on peppers, peanuts, tomatoes, ginger and bold one-pot stews. The 6 recipes gathered on this page lean on those everyday building blocks, so you can bring an authentic taste of Ethiopia to your own kitchen without hunting down hard-to-find ingredients or specialist equipment. Each one keeps the techniques approachable while staying true to the way the dish is traditionally made.
