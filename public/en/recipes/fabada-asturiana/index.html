Fabada Asturiana

Fabada asturiana is the flagship dish of Asturias, in northern Spain, a region with a wet, cold climate where a calorie-rich stew like this was essential for miners and farmers in the mountains. The name comes from "faba", the local word for the specific large white bean variety used — not just any white bean works, since smaller beans would fall apart completely during the hours of simmering the cured meats require. The "assustar" technique (to startle), repeatedly pouring cold water over the boil, is an old practice of Asturian cooks to control the beans' texture without rushing the cooking — the thermal shock prevents the thin skin from splitting, keeping the beans whole even after hours of simmering. Equally important is keeping the heat consistently gentle: a fabada cooked at high heat would produce broken beans, floating in a cloudy liquid, instead of the firm but creamy beans in a clear, rich broth from the three smoked cured meats.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 500g large white beans (fabes), soaked overnight
  • 200g Spanish chorizo
  • 200g morcilla (blood sausage)
  • 150g smoked bacon, in one piece
  • 1 whole onion, peeled
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp smoked sweet paprika
  • Salt, to taste

How to make it

  1. 1START THE POT: drain the soaked beans, put them in a large pot with cold water to cover by 5cm, add the whole onion, garlic and bay leaves.
  2. 2ADD THE MEATS: add the chorizo, morcilla and bacon, bring to a very gentle simmer, never a rolling boil.
  3. 3APPLY THE ASSUSTAR TRICK: a few times during the simmer, pour a glass of cold water into the pot, to shock the boil and prevent the beans from splitting.
  4. 4SIMMER SLOWLY: let simmer over very low heat, with the lid slightly ajar, 2-3 hours, adding warm water if it reduces too much.
  5. 5ADD THE PAPRIKA: an hour before the end, add the smoked paprika, stir gently from the side, without disturbing the beans.
  6. 6FINISH: when the beans are creamy but whole, and the meats have softened, remove the whole onion and bay leaves, salt to taste.
  7. 7SERVE: slice the meats, place them on top of the beans and serve piping hot, in a deep bowl.
