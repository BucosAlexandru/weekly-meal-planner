Flavours of Egypt

The cuisine of Egypt is built on chickpeas, olive oil, lemon, garlic and fragrant spice blends. The 6 recipes gathered on this page lean on those everyday building blocks, so you can bring an authentic taste of Egypt to your own kitchen without hunting down hard-to-find ingredients or specialist equipment. Each one keeps the techniques approachable while staying true to the way the dish is traditionally made.
