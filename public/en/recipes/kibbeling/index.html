Kibbeling

Kibbeling originally emerged as a fisherman's dish in northern Netherlands, a practical way to use cod pieces too small or irregular to be sold as whole fillets, cut into small cubes and quickly fried. The name derives from "kibbelen," a dialect word meaning to quarrel or to chop finely, referring to the technique of cutting the fish into uneven pieces. Over the last few decades, kibbeling has gone from being a local fishmonger's snack to a national phenomenon, now sold at fish stalls throughout the country, often alongside raw herring and fries. The garlic sauce that accompanies it, though it seems traditional, is actually a relatively recent addition from the 1990s, which contributed decisively to transforming kibbeling from a simple coastal snack into an emblematic Dutch dish.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 600g cod fillet, cut into 3cm cubes
  • 150g flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 200ml cold lager beer
  • Oil for frying
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 150ml mayonnaise
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste

How to make it

  1. 1PREPARE THE FISH: Cut the cod fillet into roughly 3cm cubes, pat them dry with a paper towel.
  2. 2MAKE THE BATTER: Mix the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl, gradually add the cold beer, stirring, until you get a smooth batter with the consistency of thick cream.
  3. 3PREPARE THE SAUCE: Mix the mayonnaise with the crushed garlic, chopped parsley, lemon juice, salt and pepper in a small bowl; refrigerate until serving.
  4. 4HEAT THE OIL: Heat the frying oil to 180°C in a deep pot.
  5. 5DIP THE FISH: Dip each cod piece into the batter, draining off the excess.
  6. 6FRY: Fry the pieces in small batches, 3-4 minutes, until golden and crisp, turning once.
  7. 7DRAIN: Remove the fried pieces onto paper towels, salt immediately while hot.
  8. 8SERVE: Serve immediately, with lemon wedges and the garlic sauce on the side, for dipping.
