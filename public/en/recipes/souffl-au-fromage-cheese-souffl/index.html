Soufflé au Fromage (Cheese Soufflé)

The word soufflé comes from the French verb 'to blow' — a literal description of what the dish does: it puffs up with air in the oven. The recipe appears in French cooking in the 18th century, but earned its reputation as the ultimate test of a cook's skill in the 20th century, when the fallen soufflé became a comic cliché for dinners gone wrong. What separates a successful soufflé from a failed one isn't a secret ingredient but timing: the egg whites must be folded in right before baking, and the soufflé must be eaten within minutes of leaving the oven. The structure holding it up is purely mechanical — the air trapped in the egg whites expands with heat — and it starts collapsing the moment it cools and that air contracts again.

Ingredients 4 servings

  • 40g butter (plus extra for greasing the dish)
  • 40g flour
  • 300ml warm milk
  • 150g grated gruyère (plus extra for dusting the dish)
  • 40g grated parmesan
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 5 egg whites
  • A pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. 1PREPARE THE DISH: generously butter a soufflé dish, then dust the inside with parmesan, shaking out the excess.
  2. 2Chill in the fridge.
  3. 3MAKE THE BÉCHAMEL: melt the butter in a saucepan, add the flour and stir for 1-2 minutes over low heat.
  4. 4Pour in the warm milk gradually, stirring constantly, until you get a thick, smooth sauce.
  5. 5Remove from heat.
  6. 6ADD THE CHEESE AND YOLKS: stir the gruyère, salt, pepper and nutmeg into the sauce while still warm, then add the egg yolks one at a time, mixing well after each.
  7. 7WHIP THE EGG WHITES: whip the egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff and glossy, forming firm peaks.
  8. 8FOLD IN CAREFULLY: fold a third of the egg whites into the sauce and mix vigorously to loosen it.
  9. 9Fold in the remaining whites with wide, bottom-to-top strokes, without letting them deflate.
  10. 10POUR AND LEVEL: pour the mixture into the prepared dish almost to the rim and smooth the surface with a spatula.
  11. 11Run your thumb around the inside edge of the dish to help the soufflé rise straight.
  12. 12BAKE IMMEDIATELY: bake at 190°C for 25-30 minutes, without opening the oven door, until well risen and golden on top.
  13. 13SERVE AT ONCE: remove from the oven and serve immediately, while still puffed up.
